Privacy Policy

App: Calcs · ApexTrade (package com.apextrade.calcs)
Developer: Swara Enterprises (Pankaj Gajbhiye), Wardha, Maharashtra, India
Effective date: 7 October 2026

In short: the app works completely offline. We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any of your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your own phone.

1. What the app does

Calcs · ApexTrade is a business calculator for shopkeepers and traders. You can type item calculations, group them, add tax and discount, save bills, and share a bill as text, PDF invoice or thermal receipt.

2. Information we collect

We collect nothing. The app has no user accounts, no login, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no connection to our servers. The app does not request the Internet permission.

3. Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • Your calculations, groups, saved bills and bill history.
  • Settings you choose: region, currency, tax rates, theme, sound and vibration options.
  • Shop details you may enter: shop name, phone number, address, tax/GST number, UPI ID, bank details and an optional payment QR image you pick from your gallery.

This information is used only to build your bills and invoices. It is never sent to us or to anyone else by the app on its own.

4. Sharing

A bill is shared only when you tap Share. The app then hands the text or PDF file to your phone's share menu (for example WhatsApp, e-mail, Print or Save). From that point the app you choose handles the file under its own privacy policy. We do not receive a copy.

5. Photos and files

If you choose a QR code image, the app reads only the single image you select. It does not scan or upload your gallery. Generated PDF files are saved in a temporary folder on your phone so they can be shared.

6. Third-party services

The app does not include advertising, analytics or tracking services.

7. Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

8. Data security and deletion

Your data stays in the app's private storage on your device. You can delete it at any time by deleting bills inside the app, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app. Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot recover it if you delete it or lose your phone, so please share or back up important bills yourself.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above.

10. Contact
